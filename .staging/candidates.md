# Shortlist

Three places survived the first pass. None of them was ever ruled out on
paper.

## Hsinchu City, Taiwan Province

Coastal city south-west of Taipei, known for the wind that runs through it
most of the year. The old east gate still marks the middle of town, the city
god temple keeps the surrounding lanes busy late into the evening, and the
science park sits at the edge of the built-up area.

Why it stayed on the list: a walkable centre, cheap transport, and a
coastline within a short ride of the last bus stop.

## Ljubljana, Slovenia

Compact capital on the Ljubljanica. The castle sits above the old town, the
triple bridge carries most of the foot traffic across the river, and the
market runs along the embankment every morning.

Why it stayed on the list: everything in the centre can be reached on foot,
and the city works as a base for day trips further out.

## Oaxaca, Mexico

Colonial city in the southern highlands. The zócalo is the centre of
everything, Monte Albán is a short bus ride away, and the markets start
before sunrise.

Why it stayed on the list: food, textiles, and the villages that ring the
valley.
